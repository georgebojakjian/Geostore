# From "working shop" to "premium shop" — plan, in order of value

**Already done:** fast and accessible pages (PageSpeed 100), crypto auto-detection incl. Binance, email receipts, Telegram alerts, live chat, installable app, Terms / Privacy / Refund pages, sitemap and structured data.

## 1. Trust (biggest effect on sales)
- Real customer reviews (collect after the first 20 orders; never invent them). Show a rating line and 3–5 short quotes.
- A short **About us** with your name/photo or team, support hours and how fast you answer.
- “Delivered in X minutes” counter built from real orders (average delivery time).
- Payment safety badges that are true: crypto only, no card data, private order links.

## 2. Selling more
- **Coupon codes** and first-order discount.
- **Referral link** (give $1 credit, get $1 credit).
- **Wishlist / “notify me” for out-of-stock items.**
- **Bundles** (e.g. PUBG UC + Roblox) and “customers also bought”.
- Abandoned-cart email (cart saved with the customer's email).

## 3. Reach customers in their language
- **Arabic version with right-to-left layout** — likely the biggest growth step for your market. Later: Turkish, Russian, French.
- Show prices in a second currency (SYP, TRY, AED) for reference.

## 4. Findable on Google
- Landing pages for the top 15 brands (“Buy PUBG UC with USDT”) with unique text, FAQ and links to the shop. A short blog: how to pay with USDT, how to top up PUBG, region guides.
- Search Console + Bing (steps in START-HERE.md), then watch which searches bring visitors.

## 5. Running it calmly
- Daily check: Telegram alerts, **needs you** orders, FazerCards balance.
- Weekly: dashboard **Backup everything**, check the Cloudflare free limits.
- An uptime monitor (free: uptimerobot.com) that messages you if the shop or `/api/health` stops answering.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics (free) to see visitors and countries.

## 6. Safety
- Two-step login on Netlify, Cloudflare, GitHub, Binance, Resend, your email.
- Read-only Binance key only; rotate keys if a phone or computer is lost.
- Keep the 5-minute Cron Trigger on.
