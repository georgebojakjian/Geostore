Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 09, 2026. This page is a plain-language summary for customers; it is not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Email address — to send your receipt and let you find your order.
  • Order details — items, amounts, payment reference and delivery status.
  • Account details (optional) — name, phone, country and a password stored only as a salted hash.
  • Chat messages you send us through the live chat.
  • Technical data — your IP address is used briefly for security limits (to stop abuse) and is not stored with your profile.

What we do not collect

We never ask for your wallet's private keys or seed phrase and never see your exchange login. We do not run advertising trackers and do not sell your data.

Cookies and local storage

The shop stores your cart, theme choice, chat id and sign-in token in your own browser (local storage). No advertising cookies are used.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare — runs our server and storage.
  • Netlify — hosts the website.
  • Our gift-card and top-up supplier — receives the item you buy (and the Player ID for top-ups).
  • Resend — sends receipt emails.
  • Telegram — delivers our internal order alerts and your chat messages to us.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for support, accounting and security. Unpaid orders that expire are deleted. You can ask us to delete your account.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time through the contact page.
