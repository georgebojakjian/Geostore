Supplier — FazerCards

Sell gift cards without holding any stock. Pick a product from FazerCards, set your own price, and when a customer pays you the server buys the code from FazerCards and delivers it. Your profit is your price minus their cost.

Connection

Add your FazerCards API key to Cloudflare first (see the steps in START-HERE.md → "Selling gift cards"), then press the button.

Every sale is paid from your FazerCards balance. Keep it topped up — if it runs low the shop automatically stops offering those items so nobody pays for something you cannot deliver.
