Important about Binance deposit addresses: when a customer pays from Binance to another Binance address, Binance often moves the money inside its own system — it never appears on the blockchain, so the shop cannot see it and you would have to press “Mark paid” yourself. For fully automatic payments use a wallet address you control (TronLink, Trust Wallet…) for TRC20/BEP20. The Binance Pay box above is the right choice for customers who pay inside Binance.

Publish

Two things keep your shop up to date: Sync (server: prices, codes, wallets) and Update website (names, images, new products, gift-card brands).

⚠️ Never upload this admin folder or any private*.js file to the internet — they contain your paid code.
1

Sync to server

Sends prices, private codes, wallets and your gift-card choices to your server. Price changes only need this step.

2

Update the website

Needed when you add or remove products, change names, descriptions, images or gift-card brands.

Put the file in your site folder (replace the old one), then upload the whole site folder to Netlify (Deploys → drag the folder).

3

Back up (important)

Your products live in this browser. Download a backup now and then and keep it safe.
